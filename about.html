About CampusMind

An integrated university counseling and peer support platform, built by Software Engineering students at Mbarara University of Science and Technology.

Why we built it

Getting help shouldn't be the hardest part

Students face academic stress, emotional distress, personal challenges and the pressure of adjusting to university life. Traditional counseling can be hard to reach because of scheduling, low awareness and limited communication between students and support services.

CampusMind brings counseling bookings, confidential intake, peer stories and community discussion into one accessible, student-centered platform, with the tools counselors and administrators need to run the service well.

Our objectives

What CampusMind Sets Out to Do

  • Provide a web-based system for counseling support.
  • Allow students to book counseling sessions with counselors.
  • Collect confidential intake information for support planning.
  • Promote peer support through student stories.
  • Encourage engagement through a moderated forum.
  • Give administrators tools to manage counselors and sessions.
  • Offer an appealing, functional interface with CampusMind branding.
  • Validate that the application works through testing.
How it works

Four Steps to Getting Support

Create an account

Register with your MUST registration number. Your details stay private.

Complete an intake

Share what's going on so a counselor can prepare. Urgent needs are prioritised.

Book a session

Choose a counselor and an in-person or online slot that fits your timetable.

Stay connected

Message your counselor, read peer stories and join the forum.

Who uses CampusMind

Role-Based Access

Each account type sees only the tools it needs.

Students

Book and cancel sessions, submit intake forms, share stories, post in the forum, react, report content and message counselors.

Counselors

Publish availability slots, manage sessions and notes, work through the intake queue and follow up with students.

Moderators

Review reports, approve or reject stories, pin, lock or hide discussions to keep the community safe.

Administrators

See service KPIs, manage users and roles, oversee all sessions and counselor schedules, and control the system.

Privacy & confidentiality

Your Information Is Safe With Us

  • Confidential intake. Only counseling staff can read intake forms and session notes.
  • Secure sign-in. Passwords are hashed with a unique salt and never stored in plain text. Sessions expire automatically.
  • Share anonymously. Stories, forum posts and replies can be published without your name.
  • Moderated community. Every story is reviewed before publishing and any post can be reported.
  • Limits of confidentiality. If there is a serious risk to your safety or someone else's, counselors may need to involve others to keep you safe.
